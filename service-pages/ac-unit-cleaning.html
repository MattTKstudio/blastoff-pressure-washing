Our Cleaning Process

  1. Power-Off Safety Check – Confirm the unit is safe to work on.
  2. Debris Removal – Clear leaves, dirt, and buildup from coils and casing.
  3. Fin Cleaning – Gentle brush and spray to straighten and clear airflow paths.
  4. Drain Check – Ensure condensate drainage is working correctly.
  5. Final Rinse – Restore the unit’s function and appearance.
